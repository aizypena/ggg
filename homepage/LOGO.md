# GGG — Logo Generation Prompts

Image-generation prompts for the GGG (Good Game Guild) logo, derived from `SPEC.md` (trustless tournament prize-escrow + match-verification protocol on Stellar Soroban; the contract auto-splits a pool **60/30/10 to 1st/2nd/3rd**) and `BRAND.md` (the **Titanium Carbon** aesthetic: a technical-noir settlement terminal milled from carbon fibre and titanium, lit by burnt **bronze/amber `#f7bd48`** as the protocol's single voice — brand *and* action, no second color; near-black `#131313` canvas; industrial 1px borders; milled inset shadows; low bronze glow; JetBrains Mono for data).

## Concept: "The Settlement Podium"

GGG's whole product is a **prize-escrow contract that auto-splits a pool 60/30/10 to 1st/2nd/3rd**. The mark fuses the two ideas every GGG user touches — a **winners' podium** (competitive gaming, *Good Game*) and a **stepped ledger/escrow stack** (trustless on-chain settlement). Three ascending titanium blocks read simultaneously as a podium, a payout-split bar chart, and a filling prize pool. Geometric, hard-edged, scalable to a pure silhouette — exactly the Titanium Carbon register. No text.

### Geometry guidance
- Keep bar heights roughly **1 : 1.6 : 2.4** so it reads as a real podium, not a staircase — and so the tallest bar visually anchors the deep-bronze accent stripe in the colored version.
- All three variants share the **exact same silhouette geometry**, so the colored mark can drop straight to either silhouette for dark/light contexts (favicon, nav, footer, monochrome print) without redrawing.
- Sharp 90° corners, zero border radius — structural/industrial surfaces in Titanium Carbon are `rounded-none`. No brutalist offset shadows; depth comes from a low bronze glow and a milled inset, never a hard drop block.

## Notes
- The colored mark uses **bronze as both brand and action** (per the updated `BRAND.md` thesis: "bronze is the brand and the verb"). There is no violet/second action color anymore — the only tonal variation is the bronze gradient's dark stop (`#ba880f`) on the tallest bar.
- **Text on bronze is always near-black-brown** (`#412d00`), never white — so if the mark ever sits on a bronze chip/badge, pair it with the dark silhouette, not the white one.
- Alternate direction considered and rejected: a **trophy + escrow-lock** mark (trophy cup whose bowl is a vault/lock plate). It leans further into "trustless escrow" but reads less cleanly at small sizes and ties less directly to the 60/30/10 payout that is the product's signature moment. The podium is preferred.
